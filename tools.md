### Libraries and Tools

The project will be developed in **Python**, with **Poetry** used for dependency and project management, ensuring a reproducible development environment. **Dlib** and **OpenCV** will be used for facial detection, landmark extraction, and video processing, with **CMake** supporting the compilation and installation of Dlib. **NumPy** will be used for numerical operations and manipulation of the extracted facial landmark data, while **Pandas** will support dataset organization, cleaning, and analysis. For the data science pipeline, **Scikit-Learn** will provide the machine-learning algorithms, preprocessing methods, model evaluation, and cross-validation tools, while **Joblib** will be used to save and load trained models. **TensorFlow** may additionally be used for experiments with neural-network-based models and temporal architectures.
